# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure people to share private traces or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. For a sensitive community matter, open an issue requesting a private maintainer contact without posting private details.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and related maintainer-led spaces. Maintainers are also expected to follow it.
